Privacy Notice

Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)

This Privacy Notice explains how ExpenseTracker collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

1. What We Collect

To provide our financial tracking services, we collect the following personal information:

  • Identity Information: Full name, birthdate, and ID Number.
  • Contact Information: Email address and physical address.
  • Financial Data: TIN Number, monthly income bracket, and transaction records.
  • Security Data: Passwords (hashed), security answers (hashed), and activity logs.

2. How We Protect Your Data

We implement strict security measures in compliance with the Data Privacy Act:

  • Passwords and Security Questions are secured using bcrypt hashing. They cannot be read even by system administrators.
  • Sensitive financial data (TIN) is protected using AES-256 encryption.
  • All login attempts and activities are monitored and logged to prevent unauthorized access.

3. Why We Collect It

Your data is collected solely to verify your identity, secure your account, and allow you to manage your finances effectively.

4. Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as your account is active or as required by law.

5. Your Rights

Under RA 10173, you have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of your personal data. You may contact our Data Protection Officer at dpo@expensetracker.local.
